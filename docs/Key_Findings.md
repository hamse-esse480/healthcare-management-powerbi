# Key Findings

This document summarizes the key findings explicitly presented in the Power BI report.

## 1. Patient & Appointment Analysis

### Appointment pattern by month
- **September recorded the highest share of appointments: 19.5%.**
- **February recorded the lowest share of appointments: 1.0%.**

This indicates substantial variation in appointment activity across months.

### Patient age distribution
- **Patients aged 30–44 represented the largest age group, accounting for 34% of patients.**

### Appointment status
- **51.5% of appointments were classified as either no-show or cancelled.**

This is an important operational finding because more than half of the recorded appointments fell into these two non-completed categories.

## 2. Treatment Analysis

### Treatment activity by month
- **April had the highest share of treatments: 12.5%.**
- **September had the lowest share of treatments: 5.5%.**

### Treatment type
- **Chemotherapy was the most common treatment type, accounting for 24.5% of treatments.**

### Doctor-level treatment activity
- **Sarah provided the highest number of treatments, with 46 treatments.**

## 3. Billing & Revenue Analysis

### Overall financial activity
- **Total revenue: 551.25K**
- **Total bills: 200**
- **Average bill: 2.76K**

### Monthly revenue
- Monthly revenue ranged from approximately **28K to 64K**.

### Revenue dimensions
The dashboard further breaks revenue down by treatment type, payment status, patient, and month.

## 4. Executive Overview

The Executive Overview combines the main healthcare-management indicators into a single summary view, including appointments by month, treatments by month, appointments by doctor, revenue by month, and hospital branch.

## 5. Doctor-Level Analysis

The Doctor Details page provides drillthrough analysis for individual doctors, including doctor profile, appointments by month, treatments by treatment type, appointment details, treatment analysis, appointment trends, and patient-level information.

## 6. Patient-Level Analysis

The Patient Details page provides patient-level drillthrough information, including patient information, billing history, treatment history, and appointment history.

## 7. Main Analytical Conclusions

1. **Appointment activity varied strongly by month**, with September highest (19.5%) and February lowest (1.0%).
2. **Patients aged 30–44 formed the largest patient age group**, representing 34%.
3. **No-shows and cancellations accounted for 51.5% of appointments.**
4. **Treatment activity varied by month**, with April highest at 12.5% and September lowest at 5.5%.
5. **Chemotherapy was the most common treatment type**, representing 24.5% of treatments.
6. **Sarah had the highest treatment volume**, with 46 treatments.
7. **The healthcare system recorded 551.25K in revenue across 200 bills**, with an average bill of 2.76K.
8. **Monthly revenue varied between approximately 28K and 64K.**
